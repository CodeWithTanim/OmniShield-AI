## 📌 Executive Summary & Problem Solved

Modern cybercrime is no longer limited to enterprise data centers:
1. **Citizens & Daily Wage Workers** are losing life savings to **AI-cloned voice calls** (*"Papa, I am in police trouble/hospital, send ₹50,000 right now"*) and **trick UPI "Collect Money" debit traps** disguised as rewards.
2. **Small Shopkeepers & Chaiwalas** suffer from **physical QR code tampering** (stickers pasted over merchant stands) and **fake payment screenshot apps (Paytm Spoof)** without time to read complex English security manuals.
3. **Small Factories & Startups** connect IoT sensors and use AI to write backend code quickly, resulting in **leaked cloud credentials, SQL injection, and unauthenticated machine sabotage**.

**OmniShield AI** delivers an automatic, zero-expert security shield with **audible spoken voice warnings in Hindi, Bengali, and English** that anyone can understand within **10 seconds**.

## 🏆 Key Stage-Winning Highlights

1. 📱 **Live AI Scam Call Simulator**: Realistic incoming mobile phone interface simulating *CBI Digital Arrest* and *AI Voice Clone Kidnapping* with 4-second automated regional warning sirens.
2. 📢 **OmniSound™ Smart Merchant Audio Box**: Tactile hardware replica of South Asian merchant soundboxes (Paytm/bKash), announcing fake payment screenshots and tampered QRs in Bengali, Hindi, and English.
3. 📷 **Live Camera QR Integrity Scanner**: Direct device camera viewfinder powered by `html5-qrcode` detecting malicious redirectors and `.apk` malware.
4. 📄 **1-Click 1930 Police Dossier**: Cryptographically referenced forensic incident PDF ready for instant filing on `cybercrime.gov.in`.
5. 🏆 **Judge & Stage Demo Hub**: Instant QR code handoff for judges to run OmniShield PWA directly on their own smartphones without downloading anything from an app store.
