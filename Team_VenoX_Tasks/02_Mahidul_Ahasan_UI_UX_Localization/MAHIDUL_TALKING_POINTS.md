# 🎤 Mahidul Ahasan — Hackathon Pitch & Q&A Speaking Script

> **Role**: UI/UX & Frontline Localization Lead, Team VenoX  
> **Duration**: ~35 seconds during video / live pitch

---

### 🗣️ তোমার মূল ডায়লগ (ভিডিও বা জুম কলে যখন তোমার টার্ন আসবে):

> *"Hello Judges, I am Mahidul Ahasan, UI/UX and Localization Lead for Team VenoX.*  
>  
> *Most cybersecurity tools fail because they are built in complex English for corporate IT experts. But in South Asia, the people losing money are elderly parents, chaiwalas, and busy retail cashiers.*  
>  
> *We designed OmniShield AI with a strict 'Zero-Jargon' philosophy. When someone is under panic, our app doesn't show confusing error codes — it speaks out loud in their mother tongue: Bengali, Hindi, or English.*  
>  
> *For shopkeepers, we built the OmniSound Virtual Soundbox, which announces fake payment screenshots loudly at the counter. And our Progressive Web App (PWA) requires zero app-store downloads, loading instantly on any device."*

---

### 🎨 জাজরা যদি ডিজাইন বা ইউএক্স নিয়ে প্রশ্ন করে:

* **প্রশ্ন: "Why focus so much on regional spoken voice instead of text?"**  
  **মাহিদুলের উত্তর**:  
  *"Over 40% of rural digital payment users struggle with literacy in English banking manuals. During an active scam call, victims cannot read a 10-line text disclaimer. An urgent voice alert in their regional language breaks the cognitive panic loop immediately."*

* **প্রশ্ন: "How did you design the OmniSound box?"**  
  **মাহিদুলের উত্তর**:  
  *"We replicated the exact physical soundboxes used across India and Bangladesh, with distinctive green confirmation chimes for clean payments and loud warning buzzers for fake screenshots, so shop assistants need zero training."*
