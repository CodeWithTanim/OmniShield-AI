# 🎯 Hackathon Judge Q&A Defense Cheat Sheet
> Curated by **MD Amit Hasan Robi** (Pitch & Product Lead, Team VenoX)

Use these punchy answers during live judging / Q&A sessions:

---

### Q1: "How is OmniShield AI different from Truecaller or antivirus apps?"
**Answer**:
> *"Truecaller relies on community spam numbers, which completely fails against newly registered burner SIMs and spoofed CBI numbers. Antivirus apps don't monitor physical QR standee tampering or deceptive UPI reverse-debit collect traps. OmniShield AI is multimodal: analyzing live acoustic voice frequencies, reverse-debit URI parameters, camera QR redirects, and IoT machine telemetry — all with audible spoken alerts in regional mother tongues."*

---

### Q2: "Why wouldn't shopkeepers just buy a hardware Soundbox from Paytm or PhonePe?"
**Answer**:
> *"Paytm and PhonePe soundboxes only announce transactions processed through their own specific bank QR. If a fraudster pastes a fake sticker over the standee or shows a fake payment screenshot from a spoof app, existing soundboxes remain completely silent. OmniSound AI validates physical QR integrity and detects fake payment screenshots across all banking apps with zero hardware cost."*

---

### Q3: "How do you achieve 4-second response latency for voice deepfakes?"
**Answer**:
> *"We stream acoustic frequencies at the edge using Web Audio API while the call is ongoing. Instead of waiting for full transcription, our neural vocoder detector analyzes acoustic pitch variance jitter in parallel with conversational intent classification, allowing us to trigger the warning siren in under 4 seconds."*

B2B (Shop Armor & Factory Guard) uses an affordable micro-SaaS subscription model (₹149/month for shops, ₹999/month for small factories), saving them lakhs in inventory theft and machine sabotage."*
